# My Learning Buddy – Product Requirements

- **Version:** 0.2 (Draft)
- **Platforms:** Mobile app (iOS / Android, tablet-friendly) and website
- **Audience:** Young children (about ages 2–6), with parents and teachers as supervisors

---

## 1. Overview

My Learning Buddy is an early-learning app and website that helps children learn
letters, numbers, words, shapes, days, colors and everyday things through
pictures, sounds, touch and play.

Every learning section must, at minimum, support:

- A picture or illustration for every item
- The item's name shown as text
- Audio pronunciation when the item is tapped
- Simple next/previous navigation (swipe on touch devices, arrows on the web)

## 4. Activities and Games

- **Quizzes:** "Tap the red apple", "Which one is the cow?", "Find the letter B"
- **Matching pairs:** memory card game (picture-to-picture, letter-to-word, uppercase-to-lowercase)
- **Sound to picture:** play a sound (animal, vehicle) and the child taps the matching picture
- **Counting games:** count the objects and tap the right number
- **Drag and drop:** put shapes in matching holes, sort fruits vs. vegetables, simple jigsaw puzzles
- **Coloring pages:** color in animals, vehicles, shapes, etc. with a tap-to-fill or brush tool
- Games use content from the learning sections and get harder as the child progresses

---

## 5. Rewards and Motivation

- Stars or stickers earned for completing items, tracing and games
- Sticker book / collection screen to view earned rewards
- Progress badges per section (e.g. "Alphabet Star", "Number Champ")
- Celebration animations and sounds on success
- Encouraging (never negative) feedback on wrong answers – e.g. "Try again!"

---

## 6. Audio and Language

- Letter names **and** letter sounds (phonics)
- Clear, friendly, child-appropriate voice; option to choose voice (e.g. female/male)
- Support for multiple languages (start with English; design for adding more)
- Captions: the text of what is spoken is always shown on screen
- Volume control and a mute option for music/effects separately from speech

---

## 7. Parent and Teacher Area

- Protected by a parental lock (e.g. "solve this sum" or hold-to-open) so children can't enter
- Progress reports: sections visited, items completed, quiz scores, time spent
- Screen-time limits with a gentle "time for a break" message
- Turn individual sections on or off
- Manage settings: language, voice, sound, difficulty

---

## 8. Child Profiles

- Multiple child profiles on one device / account
- Profile picker with a picture avatar (no reading needed)
- Age-based levels (e.g. 2–3, 3–4, 4–6) that adjust content and difficulty
- Progress and rewards saved per child

---

## 9. Accessibility

- Large touch targets suited to small fingers
- High-contrast mode
- Easier tracing settings (wider path tolerance, thicker guide lines)
- Dyslexia-friendly font option
- Screen-reader support where practical
- Avoid flashing or fast-moving animations; option to reduce motion

---

## 10. Privacy and Safety

- Comply with children's data-privacy laws: **COPPA** (US) and **GDPR-K** (EU/UK), plus local equivalents
- Collect as little data as possible; no personal data or analytics without verified parental consent
- No third-party ads, or only child-safe ads approved by a parent (see section 12)
- No external links, purchases or social sharing without passing the parental lock
- No chat or contact with other users
- Clear privacy policy written for parents

---

## 11. Technical Requirements

- Same content and sections on the app and the website
- Prefer one shared codebase for app and web (e.g. Flutter, or React Native + web) – to be decided
- Offline mode: content downloaded once and usable without internet
- Optional account sync of progress across devices
- Runs smoothly on low-cost tablets and older phones; small download size
- Works in portrait and landscape on phones and tablets
- Website works in modern desktop and mobile browsers
- Content (pictures, audio, word lists) stored as data, not hard-coded, so it is easy to add more

---

## 12. Business and Operations

- Pricing model: free, freemium (some sections free, rest paid), or one-time purchase / subscription – to be decided
- Follow app store rules for kids' apps (Apple Kids Category, Google Play Families policy)
- Source of images and audio: create in-house, commission, or license – confirm licensing allows commercial use
- Privacy-safe analytics to understand which sections are used (with consent)
- Process and tools for adding new content and languages after launch
- Support / feedback channel for parents
